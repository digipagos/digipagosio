1. What We Collect

DigiPagos may collect account information, contact information, login and session data, transaction metadata, device and security information, communications with support, and limited Provider-related identifiers or status information necessary to operate the integration.

DigiPagos does not collect or store government-issued identity documents or raw identity-verification data through its own onboarding flow. Where applicable, the Provider collects and processes identity and compliance information through its hosted verification process. DigiPagos may receive limited status, token, identifier, or verification-result information necessary to operate the integration and provide support.

2. How We Use It

To operate your account, process your instructions to the applicable Provider, provide support, and maintain platform security.

3. Sharing

We share the minimum information necessary with the applicable Provider to execute your requested transactions. The Provider's collection and processing of identity, compliance, payment, and transaction information is governed by the Provider's own privacy notice and terms, which you should review separately. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Our Role

Depending on the type of data and processing activity, DigiPagos may act as a business, service provider, or processor. [Final characterization pending legal review.]

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing and securing the Services, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, enforcing our agreements, and complying with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and audit obligations.

Unless a longer period is required by law, legal process, regulatory requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or a legal hold, our typical retention periods are:

  • Account and contact information: for the duration of the account relationship and up to seven years thereafter.
  • Transaction metadata and financial records: up to seven years after the relevant transaction or account activity.
  • Security, access, and technical logs: generally twelve to twenty-four months.
  • Support communications and records: generally up to seven years where needed for dispute resolution, compliance, or legal purposes.
  • Fraud, dispute, chargeback, and investigation records: until the matter is resolved and, where appropriate, up to seven years thereafter.
  • Backup copies: retained according to our backup and disaster-recovery cycles, generally for no longer than twelve months.

We do not retain government-issued identity documents or raw KYC/KYB materials when those materials are collected and retained directly by the applicable Provider. We may retain limited identifiers, verification statuses, transaction references, and audit records necessary to operate the integration, provide support, prevent fraud, and comply with legal or contractual obligations.

We may retain information for longer where required by law, regulation, tax or accounting obligations, litigation, investigation, audit, fraud prevention, or a legal hold. Where personal information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymize, or securely isolate it in accordance with our data-retention procedures.

6. Your Rights

You may request account deletion or a copy of the data DigiPagos holds about you by contacting us, subject to our legal retention obligations described above.

7. Security & Breach Notification

Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2) and never stored or logged in plain text. Session tokens are signed and transmitted only over encrypted connections. In the event of a security incident affecting personal information of Puerto Rico residents, DigiPagos will comply with applicable notification requirements, including notice to the Departamento de Asuntos del Consumidor (DACO) within the applicable timeframe.
